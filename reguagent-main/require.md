Regulatory Change-to-Action Agent
RegTech | Cross-sector enterprise • Merged and expanded from statutory-instrument and EU early-warning ideas
The real-world problem: SMEs and regulated teams receive long legal and regulatory updates but struggle to determine exactly which products, processes, contracts, training or evidence must change - and by when.
Who would pay and why: Compliance leaders, industry associations, legal and accounting firms, insurers and vertical-software providers.
Challenge statement: Build an agent that monitors authoritative regulatory sources for a defined sector, detects relevant changes, maps them to the organisation's controls and produces an owned, deadline-aware implementation plan.
Why this is agent-shaped: The system must monitor, filter by business context, compare versions, retrieve citations, ask for missing facts, open actions and verify completion evidence.
A credible two-week prototype: Choose one tightly defined sector. Demonstrate a regulatory change alert, applicability interview, control-gap analysis, task assignment and evidence log.
What success could look like:
*   Relevance and completeness of detected change
*   Accurate mapping to business obligations
*   Traceability to authoritative text
*   Action ownership and completion evidence
Possible prototype inputs: EUR-Lex, Irish Statute Book, regulator guidance and synthetic business policies or control records.
Responsible-Al and implementation boundary: Clearly separate legal text, interpretation and advice. Require qualified review and preserve source versions and effective dates.
Post-Challenge opportunity: A strong foundation for a vertical regtech startup or roles in compliance, legal technology and governance engineering.
