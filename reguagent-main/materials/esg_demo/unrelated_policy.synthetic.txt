Staff Annual Leave Policy
Effective date: 2024-01-01

1. Purpose
   This policy sets out how employees request and accrue annual leave.

2. Entitlement
   Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
   prorated for part-time staff based on contracted hours.

3. Requesting leave
   Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks
   in advance and approved by the employee's line manager.

4. Carry-over
   Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the following year with manager
   approval; unused days beyond that are forfeited.

5. Public holidays
   Public holidays observed in the bank's home jurisdiction are in addition to
   the annual leave entitlement above.

deliberately unrelated evidence sample and has nothing to do with the credit
