Helping the planet with every purchase
2025 marked the biggest year for tree commissions since our users started back in 2019. For every €1,000 spent,

they earn 1 tree. bunq Pro users plant 1 tree for every €250 spent, while bunq Elite users plant 1 tree for every €100

spent. This now totals 45 million trees, with 18 million of those being ordered in 2025 alone. This impressive

achievement not only reduces carbon emissions but also stabilizes coastlines, nurtures marine life, and supports local

communities in Kenya and Madagascar, where our reforestation efforts are making a significant impact.

Impact        Definition
Major         The incident threatens business continuity or regulatory
              compliance, including malicious data breaches in critical or
              important functions, breach of MTD, impact to >10% of clients or
              >100,000 clients, >30% of financial counterparts, >10% of daily
              transaction volume/value, duration >24 hours or >2 hours of
              downtime, or severe regulatory, financial, or reputational
              consequences.

High          The incident significantly disrupts operations or customers,
              impacting critical or non-critical functions beyond RTO, affecting
              >1,000 users (critical) or >10,000 users (non-critical), causing
              financial loss ≥ €100,000, a GDPR-reportable non-malicious data
              breach, or substantial reputational damage requiring senior
              management escalation.

Medium        The incident causes material operational or customer impact,
              including any actual function downtime, complete Eva
              unavailability, partial disruption of critical or important functions,
              financial loss between €50,000 and €100,000, a sensitive data
              breach, or visible reputational impact such as customer complaints
              or niche media coverage.

Low           The incident has limited and contained impact, affecting
              moderately important features or 10–1,000 Evas, with no function
              downtime or full Eva loss, financial loss between €5,000 and
              €50,000, no sensitive data breach, and minimal reputational
              impact.

Hiccup        The incident has negligible impact, affecting low-importance
              features or fewer than 10 Evas, with no downtime, no Eva usability
              loss, no data breach, financial loss below €5,000, no public
              attention, and quick resolution through normal operations.

contractual view treats all on-demand deposits as overnight liabilities, regardless of their
historical stability, while the most stable portion of those funds is invested for longer
periods. .

We estimate the stability of our user balances via a non-maturing deposit model. The model
was redeveloped in 2025 to establish a more robust estimate of potential liquidity needs.
The new methodology tracks the run-off of existing user balances irrespective of net inflows
from other users, ensuring bunq can support its investment strategy with existing funds
only.

Capital management

The Group performs a regular internal capital adequacy assessment at least once a year,
with a forward-looking horizon of at least three years. Its starting point is the business
growth plan, a risk assessment, and the definition of a risk appetite. Based on the outcomes,
the Group sets its capital requirements.

These projections are regularly reviewed and updated. Such projections include required
capital injections to remain compliant with relevant rules such as the capital requirements
from the regulator. This allows for short- and longer-term management of capital. The
capital requirement mainly consists of capital held to fund balance sheet growth taking into
account the Group’s financial forecasts under different scenarios, also including stress
scenarios. Secondly the capital requirement is determined by the asset-management
activities performed by the Group, resulting in Risk Weighted Assets.

The Group has a funding plan in place which describes the measures available To meet
future capital requirements. For the situation that no capital funds are available the Group
has developed a plan to manage the growth rate. As at both reporting dates, and on a
quarterly basis throughout the year, the Group complied with the externally imposed
capital requirements.

Outsourcing risk

performance and risk management. The risk may materialize due to inadequate delivery of

Management of outsourcing risk

              future cash flows, the value of collateral and the assessment of whether a default has
              occurred with regard to loans and advances to customers. The use of different
              assumptions could produce significantly different estimates of the expected credit loss
              allowance. The modeling assumptions are disclosed in A. Credit Risk in the Risk
              Management section of the annual report.
