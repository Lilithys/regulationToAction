2. Highlights 2025: Impact in Action

    2.1 A Greener World: 18 Million Trees and Counting

At bunq, we build tools that empower our users to make a positive impact
on the world with every tap of their card. In 2025, this philosophy translated
into our most significant environmental achievement to date. Our
community has pledged an incredible 18 million trees with our partner
Veritree, marking the largest single-year contribution since we launched
our green-enabling feature in 2019.

This milestone is a direct result of our users' everyday spending. The
initiative is simple: by using their cards, users help reforest the planet. Our
tiered plans, such as bunq Pro and Elite, allow them to accelerate this
impact, planting a tree for every €250 and €100 spent, respectively.

More Than Just Trees

Through our partnership with Veritree, bunq’s reforestation efforts go
beyond planting trees - they help restore entire ecosystems while
supporting the people who depend on them. In East Africa, mangrove
projects engage local communities in protecting and maintaining coastal
environments, combining traditional knowledge with proven planting
techniques to strengthen long-term survival rates. Early biodiversity signals
and continuous monitoring show that these restoration efforts are
contributing to healthier, more resilient natural habitats.

The impact is social as well as environmental. With over 60% of staff
involved in these projects being women, restoration activities create
inclusive economic opportunities while building local stewardship of
natural resources. By supporting community-led conservation, bunq
ensures that every tree planted contributes not only to carbon capture, but
also to stronger livelihoods, empowered communities, and lasting
environmental resilience.
